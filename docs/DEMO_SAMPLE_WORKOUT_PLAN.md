## Program

Name: Strength and Hypertrophy Foundation
Duration: 6 weeks
Goal: Build general strength, controlled hypertrophy, and better exercise consistency.
Audience: Beginner-to-intermediate trainee with access to a normal gym.

## Day 1: Upper Body Strength

Notes: Keep reps controlled. If shoulder feels irritated, use a neutral-grip dumbbell press instead of barbell bench. فارسی: تمرکز روی فرم و کنترل حرکت.

1. Bench Press
   - Sets: 4
   - Reps: 6-8
   - Rest: 120 seconds
   - RPE: 7-8
   - Note: Pause briefly on the chest. Do not bounce the bar.

2. Lat Pulldown
   - Sets: 3
   - Reps: 8-10
   - Rest: 90 seconds
   - RPE: 7
   - Note: Pull elbows down toward ribs. کنترل شانه ها.

3. Seated Cable Row
   - Sets: 3
   - Reps: 10-12
   - Rest: 90 seconds
   - RPE: 7
   - Note: Keep torso still and squeeze upper back.

## Day 2: Lower Body Controlled Strength

Notes: Controlled tempo. Stop if sharp pain appears. Mild muscle fatigue is fine.

1. Leg Press
   - Sets: 4
   - Reps: 8-10
   - Rest: 120 seconds
   - RPE: 7-8
   - Note: Feet shoulder-width. Do not lock knees hard at the top.

2. Romanian Deadlift
   - Sets: 3
   - Reps: 8
   - Rest: 120 seconds
   - RPE: 7
   - Note: Hinge from hips, soft knees, neutral back.

3. Goblet Squat
   - Sets: 3
   - Reps: 10-12
   - Rest: 90 seconds
   - RPE: 7
   - Note: Use a dumbbell or kettlebell. فارسی: عمق حرکت تا جایی که فرم خوب بماند.

## Day 3: Full Body / Conditioning Support

Notes: This day should feel productive, not crushing. Keep rest honest and technique clean.

1. Dumbbell Shoulder Press
   - Sets: 3
   - Reps: 8-10
   - Rest: 90 seconds
   - RPE: 7
   - Note: Seated or standing. Brace ribs down.

2. Plank
   - Sets: 3
   - Reps: 30-45 seconds
   - Rest: 60 seconds
   - RPE: 6-7
   - Note: Straight line from shoulder to ankle. نفس آرام.

3. Incline Treadmill Walk
   - Sets: 1
   - Reps: 10-15 minutes
   - Rest: 0 seconds
   - RPE: 6
   - Note: Finish with steady breathing. No sprinting required.
